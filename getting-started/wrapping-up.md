# Wrapping up

### Short-term leave

* Inform any relevant people of your absence (PI, collaborators, co-authors)
* Make a backup of your local machine and of your files on TACC. Create a metadata and list to-do for when you return.
* Check for any relevant deadlines (e.g., fellowships, TACC allocations, manuscript deadlines, grant deliverables).
* If traveling internationally, check that you have a working VPN

### Long-term or permanent leave

* Backup your local machine. Send or leave a copy of this back up to the PI.
* Return any university or group property (e.g., books, computers) or discuss with PI.
* Backup and clean up your files on TACC. Remove unneeded files and zip files.
* Pack personal items and clean desk space.
* Published work:&#x20;
  * DOI links are up-to-date
  * Repository drafts are up-to-date
  * Data and metadata are deposited on the group repository on TACC (location in [group accounts](https://utexas.box.com/s/217scejibg75iq5k05uocjtuqsa8rrvi))
  * Discuss with PI if a continued university affiliation would make sense
* Update your profile on thepermanent group website
